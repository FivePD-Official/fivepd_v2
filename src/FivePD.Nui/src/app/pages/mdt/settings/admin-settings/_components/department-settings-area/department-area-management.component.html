In order to add an area to a department, press the Edit button in its row, then select the Add area coordinates button, and click on the map to add a coordinate.

Right click on a coordinate marker to remove it.

You can also drag the coordinate markers to change their position, when the Edit mode is enabled. Note that you cannot drag them while adding a new coordinate.
